# Credits

The code is MIT (LICENSE). It reads dictionary files built by ccez-lang
from these sources; apps that ship or download them must credit them:

- **JMdict** by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group
  (EDRDG), CC BY-SA 4.0: Japanese entries. https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
- **CC-CEDICT** by MDBG, CC BY-SA 4.0: Chinese entries and pinyin.
  https://cc-cedict.org/wiki/
- **Wiktionary**, through kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen's wiktextract),
  CC BY-SA 4.0: entries for French, German, Spanish, Korean and the
  others. https://kaikki.org
- **IPAdic** (NAIST / IPA), its own BSD-style licence: the Japanese
  analyser's dictionary, used by lindera (MIT).
- **Kanjium** by mifunetoshiro, CC BY-SA 4.0: Japanese pitch accents.
  https://github.com/mifunetoshiro/kanjium
- **wordfreq** by Robyn Speer, CC BY-SA 4.0: word frequencies (word
  lists the apps build). https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq

Test fixtures (tests/fixtures) are a few entries in those formats,
written for the tests; the CC-CEDICT lines follow CC-CEDICT and are
CC BY-SA 4.0.
